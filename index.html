Cliperstream

Cliperstream is a small personal tool used by its owner to prepare short video clips and to follow the performance of the videos published on the owner's own TikTok account.

With the TikTok Display API (scopes user.info.basic and video.list), the tool reads, only after the owner has logged in and given permission, the public statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares). The data is stored on the owner's own computer and is not shared, sold or published.

The tool does not post videos to TikTok and does not access any other user's account.
