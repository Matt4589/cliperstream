Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What data is used

After the Owner logs in with TikTok and grants permission, Cliperstream reads: basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, avatar) and the list of the Owner's own videos with their public statistics (video.list: title, description, duration, cover image, views, likes, comments, shares).

How it is used

The data is used only to show the Owner how their own videos perform. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored locally on the Owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or published. Access tokens are kept in a local file and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services).

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the Owner deletes the local files. To delete data or ask a question, contact mathias.moles@gmail.com.
